The Educational Crisis & Our Vision

India continues to face stern challenges. Despite growing investment in education, severe gaps remain between urban and rural infrastructures.

35%

Illiteracy Rate

35% of the population is still illiterate; only 15% of Indian students reach high school, and just 7% graduate.

25%

Vacant Teaching Posts

25% of teaching positions nationwide are vacant, and 57% of college professors lack either a master's or PhD degree.

5-6 km

Daily Commute

With daily treks of 5-6 km to under-equipped schools lacking proper facilities, young learners spend hours commuting only to sit in classrooms devoid of proper infrastructure.

Our Comprehensive Mission

  • Equitable Education: Provide a uniform standard of education all across the entire country (rural or urban).
  • Vernacular Preservation: Counteract the rapid decline of local language schools caused by the shift towards ICSE and CBSE curricula, preventing the loss of native language proficiency.
  • Empowering Farmers: Teach new techniques like rain water harvesting, raised bed farming, creating organic fertilizers, and implementing biogas. We want to see a software engineer in every farmer and a farmer in every software engineer.
  • Single-Teacher Solutions: Prevent scenarios where one teacher shoulders the responsibility of teaching multiple subjects to multiple grades by automating lectures aligned with school timetables.

What makes it so unique and affordable?

Normally you need 3 units: a projector, a PC, and an electronic exam system. Eprashala combines the software, projector with built-in PC, and electronic examination system for 60 students at 1/4th the cost of competitors. The device dimensions are 320mm * 240mm * 120mm and weigh only 4.5 kgs.

Why can't other projectors run on solar?

Conventional projectors use mercury vapor lamps requiring high starting voltages (nearly 1000 volts) and consume around 300 watts AC. Running them on solar requires massive panels and 450W DC to AC inverters, driving the cost to almost Rs. 2 Lakhs.

Is the internal PC powerful enough?

The PC built inside uses an Intel ultra low power CPU, a fanless CPU with only 15 watts of power consumption. Running Windows XP on an 8 GB high-speed Solid State Disk is as fast as an Intel Atom 1.8 ghz on a conventional hard drive. The secondary storage is a 64 gb nand disk.

Reliability in Indian Climates?

Designed to sustain 90% relative humidity, 45-degree hot summers, and extremely dusty conditions. As per our design, it should run at least 5 years without any service calls.
