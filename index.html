Built for Extreme Climates.
Powered by the Sun.

Eprashala is a compact 1.5 kg device operating on a mere 50 watts of LED power, drawing less than 100 watts of DC power supplied by solar panels[cite: 1]. Conventional systems use mercury vapor lamps running at 300°C, requiring heavy AC inverters[cite: 2]. Our fanless AMD Geode CPU architecture on SSD runs remarkably cool (Max 70°C) and requires absolutely zero maintenance[cite: 2].
